GOOD TO KNOW

Why did I see an XML file?

RSS is a standard data format. Browsers often show its raw XML text, but news-reader apps turn it into a normal list of headlines and links.

The XML is not an error, and you don't need to download or open the file yourself.

03 / COMMON QUESTIONS

THE DETAILS.

Will I get push notifications?

That depends on your RSS reader and its settings. VICEWIRE itself does not send notifications.

How fresh is the feed?

GitHub Actions checks sources approximately every three hours. Scheduled runs can be delayed; your reader also chooses its own polling interval.

Does it cost anything?

The feed is free to access. Some third-party RSS apps may offer optional paid features.

Does this cover every post on X?

No. Publicly indexed X posts are incomplete, and VICEWIRE does not have comprehensive X API access.
