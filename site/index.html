There is no independent safety clearance of the specific executable now being discussed in community posts in the reporting reviewed here. Security researchers have documented malicious GTA VI-themed impostor downloads, but those are separate samples. Don't mistake their results for a test of Cyberleek's particular file.

What would help verify safety? A precise cryptographic hash, independently published malware analysis by reputable researchers, and corroborating evidence about the exact file's behavior. Comments saying “works for me” or a single clean antivirus result are not proof.
